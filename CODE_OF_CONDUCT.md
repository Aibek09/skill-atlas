# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal characteristics.
- Accept feedback gracefully and help others learn.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and project boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, spam, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this code. To report a concern, use the private security-reporting option described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) when available, or contact the repository owner privately through their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant and intentionally kept concise for this project.
